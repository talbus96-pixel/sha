    About & sound credits

    Breathe is a calm paced-breathing guide. Follow the moving dot: it rises as you breathe in, falls as you breathe out, and travels sideways while you hold. Everything runs on your device. This app is not medical advice.

    Ambient recordings (via the open-source Blanket project):

    • Rain — alex36917 (CC BY)
    • Ocean — Luftrum (CC BY)
    • Stream — gluckose (CC0)
    • Forest — kvgarlic (CC0)
    • Fireplace — ezwa (public domain)
    • Night — Lisa Redfern (public domain)
    • Wind — felix.blume (CC0)

    Recordings were edited by the Blanket project and re-encoded and volume-matched for this app. Bowl, chime and tone cues are synthesised live.
